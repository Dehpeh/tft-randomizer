Restriction Randomizer

Rank sets how many handicaps you carry. The gamemaster rolls them, the lobby sees them, and every result is seeded so it can be checked afterwards.

/ RUNNING A LOBBY

A gamemaster opens a lobby and hands out the link. Players sign in, get rolled, play, and the gamemaster submits the placements at the end.

/ YOUR RECORD

My matches
/ ROLLING ON YOUR OWN

The same engine with no account and no server, rolling in your browser. For testing restrictions in customs before they reach a real lobby.

Solo roller
/ DISTRIBUTION BY RANK

Who gets what

Rolled fresh for every player, every game. Two similar restrictions in one roll → the lower one is rerolled automatically.

/ NOTES

Restrictions and the rank distribution come straight from the tournament doc. Both are subject to change — the list lives in lib/restrictions.js, one line per restriction, and the family tag is what decides which pairs count as “similar” for the reroll rule.
