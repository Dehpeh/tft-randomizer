Proctor beta

Share your game window and this page catches every augment screen as it happens, with a screenshot, next to what the randomizer told you to take. One glance for an umpire instead of a stream-watching shift.

/ WHAT IT CATCHES

Augment screens, by the words on them. Tested against four hours of real games it found every one — three a game — and nothing false in between. It does not try to say which card you clicked: there is nothing in the picture that reliably tells, so the screenshot goes to a person instead.

/ WHERE IT GOES

It never touches the game — this is the browser feature a video call uses. The recording never leaves your machine: what goes to your gamemaster, when you press send, is a note and a single still of the moment it caught. Everything it claims is measured at the lab before it is believed.

/ SETUP

Pick the TFT window itself rather than the whole screen — less to watch, and nothing else of yours is ever in frame. Once it is running, Clip this moment grabs the frame for anything the proctor does not watch for by itself — a carousel, a shop, whatever the argument is about. Press C instead if you are mid-game.

Findings go to your gamemaster as they happen. There is no send button and nothing to delete: when the proctor notes a moment, that note and the screenshot of your game window at that moment are on their way before you see them. It is a cash-prize tournament, and evidence you could choose to withhold would not be worth much.

What stays yours: whether it watches at all. Stop the share and nothing further is captured. Your video is never uploaded — frames are measured and thrown away, and only the still images of flagged moments are sent.
